# ATL-STUDENT-LIBRARY
# 📚 My ATL Digital Library Project

Welcome! This is a digital library project that I am building as part of the **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)** program. 

I am using this GitHub repository to:
1. Learn how programming and version control work.
2. Easily transfer my project files between school and home.
3. Get feedback, suggestions, and feature ideas from my mentors and classmates.

---

## 🛠️ About the Project

* **What it does:** A digital space to organize, share, and access learning resources.
* **Built using:** HTML, CSS
* **Current Stage:** Work in Progress 🚀

---

## 📂 How to Explore the Files

You don't need any special software to look at the project!
* Just click on the folders and files listed above on this page to view the code, designs, and notes.
* If you need to download a file to your computer, click on the file, click the **three dots (...)** on the top right, and select **Download**.

---

## 💬 How to Give Feedback & Ideas (Classmates & Mentors)

I would love to hear your thoughts, ideas, or feature requests! You can share them with me right here on GitHub:

1. Click on the **"Issues"** tab at the top of this page (it has a little circle icon next to it).
2. Click the green **"New Issue"** button.
3. Give your idea a title (e.g., *Suggestion: Add a search bar*).
4. Write your feedback in the box and click **"Submit new issue"**.

Thank you for visiting my project and helping me learn! 🌟
